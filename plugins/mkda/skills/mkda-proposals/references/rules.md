# MKDA basics for proposals

The user's layer wins over this page.

## Style

- 16:9 slides, 1280×720.
- Real photos of the client, full page or tall columns. No good photos: typography only.
- The user's official logo, dark or light version as the background needs.
- Footer: logo and page number only.
- Full, merged pages; the number of pages depends on the services.

## Words

- Speak to the client's company as a whole, in the form of address the profile asks for.
- Short, simple sentences. No em dashes.
- Facts about the client come from their pages or from the user, never invented.
- Times, exact quantities, support hours: only if the user gave them.
- Never name or criticise the client's current supplier: say what is missing today and what is added.
- References and case studies only if the user provided them and may show them.

## Prices

- Every figure comes from the user and is the same on every page.
- Package prices, with what is included. No unit prices, crossed-out prices or discounts unless the user asks.
- No promised results and no return-on-investment sums.
- Advertising: the budget paid to the platforms is written apart from the fee.
- Always state currency, whether tax is included, payment terms and validity.
