# Pages

`assets/template.html` has the CSS and one example of each page. CSS classes in brackets.

Always:

1. **Cover** (`.photo-bg.cover`): photo, logo, title about the client, who it is for, date. Without a photo remove `.ph`.
2. **The project** (`.proj`): where the client is and why now. Two short paragraphs, up to three true key numbers (`.stats`).
3. **How we work** (`.flow`): the steps, who does what. A sample calendar only for recurring content, with real dates: check the weekday of the 1st.
4. **What you get and price** (`.groups`, `.deal`, `.plans`): one or two pages per service.
5. **Summary** (`.end`): every figure in one table, up to three frequent questions, validity, signature and contacts.

Merge what fits on one page. Two packages: `.plans` on a dark page. One package: `.deal`.
