# MKDA basics for content

The user's layer wins over this page.

## Teach one thing

1. One idea per piece. Three tools or concepts are three posts.
2. Decide first what the reader can do at the end; everything serves that.
3. The cover says the promise, to the reader, with the name of the thing.
4. Slide 2 says what we are talking about and why it matters to them.
5. Titles read in a row make sense without images.
6. Write for someone who has never heard of it. Explain each technical word the first time, two at most, with an everyday comparison that works like the real thing.
7. One example goes through the whole piece, step by step, next to the real screen or photo.
8. Count steps when the topic allows it. End with something to do in under 5 minutes.
9. One rule or number a beginner would send to a friend.
10. The reel explains why, the carousel says what to do. They do not repeat each other.

## Words

- Titles: two lines at most (three on the cover), broken where the sense breaks. One sentence of 14 words at most under a title.
- It must sound like the user talking to a friend. Read it aloud.
- Positive: a limit is told as an opportunity. No fear, no shaming.
- Correct accents and spelling, no em dashes.
- No invented numbers, no results promised, no sources on slides (they stay in `research.md`).
- Everyday words over technical ones. Avoid machine habits: lists of three everywhere, "not only... but also", one-word questions, sentences all the same length.
- When the topic is a category, name several tools, not one.

## Images

- Real ones: the user's photos, official screens, free-licence photos with the licence checked. Keep the source in `research.md`.
- No AI image in place of something real. A diagram only for a concept with no screen, one per post.
- A screen is shown whole at least once, at least 900 px wide; then zoom on the point.
- Every image teaches something: if removing it changes nothing, remove it.
- No other people's trademarks, faces or private data unless the user may show them.

## Shape

- Carousel: 6-7 slides, up to 10 when the topic starts from zero. Cover, what it is, the content one thing per slide, recap to save, close.
- Stories: 5-6, at most 20 words each, room for stickers, a visible thread. They go over the carousel in practical bits.
- Reel: 20-30 seconds, silent by default so the user picks the music.
- Two or three carousel slides and stories may be short videos, where movement shows something.
