# Captions

Two per job, different from each other.

- **Carousel**: 90-160 words. **Reel**: 40-90 words, a different first line.
- First two lines: the most important thing and the words people would search, said naturally.
- One line worth a screenshot or sending to a friend.
- Short paragraphs. No hashtags by default. One closing invitation, tied to the topic.
- Change shape each time: scene, figure, question and answer, myth and truth, letter, steps, before and after.
- No "comment WORD", no "tag a friend", unless the user's rules want them.
- Write three first lines and keep the clearest. Do not repeat sentences from the slides.

Other networks: same content, the length and manners of that network. Verify its current limits today.
